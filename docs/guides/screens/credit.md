:::lead
When you visit a network that charges visitors, your requests are paid from your own network's credit there. Credit shows that balance, the price of each model, what this machine spent, and lets you ask the network's admin for more.
:::

:::steps
### See the balance
The balance belongs to your home network: every machine of it that visits draws on the same account.
### Check the prices
Prices are per 1000 input and output tokens. A model that is not listed is free.
### Ask for credit
Pay the network's admin the way you agreed, then send a request with the amount and a note they can recognize. The balance rises once they approve it.
:::

:::note When the credit runs out
A request the balance cannot cover is refused before it runs, and the Chat says so with a link here. Nothing is charged for a refused request.
:::
